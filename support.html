Common questions

It doesn't start when I whisper.

Try switching to "Permissive" in Settings → Sensitivity. WhisperNote needs a little bit of breath-noise to fire — the quieter the room, the easier it picks up the whisper. Holding the phone close to your mouth helps. If a fan is on, you may want to point the bottom mic away from it.

It starts when I talk normally.

Switch to "Strict" in Settings → Sensitivity. The strict preset requires a higher proportion of high-frequency turbulent noise (the whisper signature) before activating.

Where do my transcripts come from?

Apple's Speech framework, running locally on your iPhone with on-device recognition. They never leave the device.

Why isn't there an iCloud sync?

By design. WhisperNote ships with zero network access. Adding iCloud would mean we'd have to audit, document, and disclose data flow we'd rather not have.

Where is my history stored?

On your device, in SwiftData. Nothing is sent anywhere. Deleting the app removes everything.

Found a bug?

Drop us an email with: iOS version, what you whispered (or were trying to), and what happened instead. Screenshots help.
